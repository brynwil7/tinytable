TinyTable

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026
Last Updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tiny Table LLC ("TinyTable," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, stores, processes, and shares information when you access or use the TinyTable mobile application and related services (collectively, the "App").

TinyTable is designed to help parents and authorized adult caregivers track, understand, and organize nutrition information for children from approximately 6 months through 5 years of age.

We understand that information about a child's food intake, weight, nutrition, and development is personal. We take privacy seriously and aim to collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide the App's features.

By using TinyTable, you acknowledge that you have reviewed this Privacy Policy.

1. Who Can Use TinyTable

TinyTable is intended exclusively for individuals who are at least 18 years old.

Children are not permitted to create accounts, register, independently access, or operate the App. TinyTable accounts are created and managed by adults, including parents and authorized caregivers.

An adult may enter information about a child for personal family-management purposes. The child is not the account holder.

By creating an account or using TinyTable, you represent that:

  • You are at least 18 years old.
  • You are the child's parent, legal guardian, or an adult authorized by the parent or legal guardian to manage the child's information.
  • You have the authority to provide the information you submit.
  • You will not allow a child to independently access or operate the App.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, TinyTable may collect or process the following categories of information.

A. Account Information

When you create an adult account, we may collect:

  • Name or display name.
  • Email address.
  • Authentication information provided through Apple, Google, or email sign-in.
  • Account identifiers.
  • Account preferences and settings.
  • Account creation and activity information.

If you sign in through Apple or Google, those providers may share certain account information with us according to your sign-in choices and their privacy policies.

We do not receive your Apple or Google password through their standard authentication services.

B. Child Profile Information

Adults may enter information about children they manage, including:

  • First name or nickname.
  • Date of birth.
  • Age.
  • Weight.
  • Feeding and dietary information.
  • Food preferences.
  • Foods introduced or consumed.
  • Milk intake, including breast milk, formula, and other milk.
  • Vitamins and supplements.
  • Allergies or dietary restrictions, if entered.
  • Other information voluntarily provided by the adult.

A child profile is not an independent user account. It is information managed by an adult account holder.

C. Nutrition and Health-Related Information

To provide nutrition tracking and meal-planning features, TinyTable may process:

  • Daily food intake.
  • Meal and snack records.
  • Estimated calories and macronutrients.
  • Protein, carbohydrates, fats, fiber, and sugars.
  • Vitamins and minerals, including iron, calcium, vitamin A, vitamin C, and vitamin D.
  • Sodium and other nutritional measurements.
  • Milk and formula intake.
  • Supplement information.
  • Nutritional summaries and reports.
  • Calculated or estimated nutrient intake.
  • Recommendations generated from information entered into the App.

Some of this information may qualify as consumer health data under applicable state privacy laws.

D. Meal Planning and App Activity

We may collect or process:

  • Saved recipes.
  • Meal plans.
  • Suggested meals.
  • Favorite foods.
  • Shopping or grocery lists, if available.
  • Food searches.
  • Barcode scans.
  • Product information.
  • Reminder preferences.
  • App settings and feature preferences.

E. Payment and Subscription Information

TinyTable offers paid subscription options.

Subscriptions may be processed through Apple's App Store and subscription-management providers such as RevenueCat.

We may receive information such as:

  • Subscription status.
  • Subscription type.
  • Purchase and renewal dates.
  • Transaction or entitlement identifiers.
  • Trial eligibility.
  • Subscription expiration or cancellation status.

Payments are handled by Apple. Payment card information is handled by the applicable payment provider. TinyTable does not intentionally collect or store full payment card numbers or payment security codes.

F. Technical and Device Information

Depending on your device, operating system, and the services used to operate the App, certain technical information may be processed, including:

  • Device type and operating system.
  • App version.
  • Authentication and session identifiers.
  • IP address.
  • Error and diagnostic information.
  • Security logs.
  • Network and connection information.

We do not intentionally use precise location tracking, contact-list access, or advertising identifiers for behavioral advertising.

The camera is used only to scan product barcodes. TinyTable does not save photos or video.

If these practices change, we will update this Policy as appropriate.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to operate, maintain, and improve TinyTable.

This includes:

  • Creating and managing adult accounts.
  • Creating and maintaining child profiles.
  • Recording food and milk intake.
  • Calculating estimated nutritional values.
  • Displaying nutritional summaries and reports.
  • Providing age-appropriate meal suggestions.
  • Generating recipes and meal plans.
  • Saving favorite foods and recipes.
  • Supporting barcode scanning and food searches.
  • Managing reminders and app preferences.
  • Synchronizing information across authorized devices.
  • Providing family-sharing functionality.
  • Managing subscriptions and subscription access.
  • Responding to customer support requests.
  • Protecting accounts and preventing unauthorized access.
  • Troubleshooting technical issues.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information or consumer health data for monetary consideration.

We do not use children's nutrition information to create targeted advertising profiles.

TinyTable provides general nutrition information, not medical advice. Talk with your child's pediatrician about their health, growth, allergies and supplements.

4. Artificial Intelligence and Personalized Recommendations

Certain TinyTable features may use artificial intelligence or automated systems to help generate meal suggestions, recipes, food recommendations, or other nutritional content.

When these features are used, information necessary to generate the requested result may be processed by our AI service provider.

Depending on the feature, this information may include:

  • Child age or age range.
  • Food preferences.
  • Dietary restrictions.
  • Foods already entered into the App.
  • Nutritional goals or relevant nutritional information.
  • Other information necessary to generate the requested recommendation.

We aim to limit information sent to AI providers to what is reasonably necessary for the requested feature.

AI PROVIDER: TinyTable does not currently use any artificial intelligence provider, and no information is sent to one. If we add AI features, we will name each provider here and obtain any consent required by law before information is sent.

The provider's processing of information is subject to its applicable terms, privacy practices, and our contractual arrangements where applicable.

We will not represent that information is excluded from AI-provider model training unless that exclusion is confirmed by the provider's terms and applicable settings or contractual commitments.

AI-generated recommendations are informational only. They are not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

5. Cloud Storage and Synchronization

Current storage: Today, TinyTable stores all App data only on your device, inside the App's private storage, protected by your device's passcode encryption. Accounts, cloud storage and family sharing described in this Policy are planned features that are not yet active. We will update this Policy and ask for any required consent before they are turned on.

TinyTable may use cloud infrastructure to store account information, child profiles, meal records, nutrition information, preferences, and other App data.

Cloud storage allows authorized adults to access information across supported devices and enables family-sharing functionality.

CLOUD DATABASE / HOSTING PROVIDER: Not yet in use. Today all App data is stored only on your device. We will name our cloud database and hosting provider here, and obtain any consent required by law, before cloud storage is turned on.

Information stored in the cloud may be processed by our hosting and infrastructure providers acting on our behalf.

We take reasonable measures to protect information during transmission and storage. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Family Sharing and Authorized Caregivers

TinyTable may allow an adult account holder to invite up to four additional adults to access shared child profiles and related information, for a total of five people including the account holder.

Family-sharing participants must be at least 18 years old.

The adult who creates or manages a child profile is responsible for ensuring that invited individuals are authorized to access the information.

Depending on the permissions and features available, invited adults may be able to view or contribute to:

  • Child profile information.
  • Food and milk records.
  • Nutrition summaries.
  • Meal plans.
  • Saved recipes.
  • Other information made available through family sharing.

The primary account holder may manage invitations and revoke access where supported.

Revoking a person's access prevents future authorized access through the shared account, but it may not automatically remove information that the person previously viewed, downloaded, copied, or independently retained.

We encourage account holders to invite only trusted adults.

  • Request a copy of your information.

11. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your TinyTable account and associated information by using the available account-deletion feature or contacting us at tinytable.help@gmail.com.

In the App, Account → Privacy & data → Delete all my data immediately and permanently removes your children, logs, plans and settings stored on your device. Once accounts and cloud storage exist, deleted information will be removed from our active servers within 30 days, apart from temporary backups that are overwritten on their normal cycle.

When we receive a valid deletion request, we will take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify information associated with the account, including child profiles and related nutrition records, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Where family-sharing functionality is enabled, deletion may affect shared access and associated records. We will explain any material consequences before completing deletion where reasonably practicable.

Deleting the App from your device does not necessarily delete your account or cloud-stored information.

Deleting your TinyTable account does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription. You must separately manage or cancel your subscription through the applicable payment provider.

12. Children's Privacy and COPPA

TinyTable is designed for adults aged 18 and older. Children are not permitted to create accounts, register, or independently use the App.

Parents and authorized adult caregivers may enter information about children to manage family nutrition and feeding records.

We do not knowingly solicit information directly from children or knowingly permit children to create accounts.
