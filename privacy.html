TinyTable

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Who we are

TinyTable is an app that helps parents and caregivers track what their baby or toddler eats. TinyTable is used by adults. Children do not use the app or create accounts.

Questions about privacy: tinytable.help@gmail.com.

What we collect

About your child (entered by you): first name or nickname, birthday, weight, profile color, and the foods, milk, vitamins and supplements you log, along with the nutrition worked out from them.

About you: your plan choice, meal plans, recipes, favorites, scanned products and reminder settings. When accounts are added, your sign-in email or Apple or Google sign-in ID.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising ID or anything outside the app. The camera is used only to read barcodes; no pictures are saved.

How we use it

Only to run TinyTable for you: showing your child's daily intake, building meal plans, reports, reminders and scan scores.

We do not sell your data or your child's data, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. We do not use tracking or analytics tools that follow you across apps.

Where it is stored

Right now everything is stored on your phone, inside TinyTable's private storage, and protected by your phone's passcode encryption.

When accounts and family sharing are added, your data will also be stored with our database provider in the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest, and only you and the family members you invite will be able to see it. We will update this policy and ask for your consent before that happens.

Who else receives information

To look up nutrition labels, TinyTable sends only the words you search or the barcode you scan to public food and supplement databases (USDA FoodData Central, Open Food Facts and the NIH Dietary Supplement Label Database). Nothing about you or your child is sent with these searches.

Payments are handled by Apple. When added, subscriptions will be managed by our billing provider and accounts by our database provider, which may process your information only to provide those services to us.

We will share information if the law requires it, for example a valid court order.

Your choices and rights

You can see, change or remove anything you have logged at any time.

In Account → Privacy & data you can download a copy of your data or delete all of it. Deleting removes your children, logs, plans and settings from TinyTable permanently.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your data. You can turn reminders and camera access on or off in the app and in your phone's Settings.

To make a request or ask a question, email tinytable.help@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days. Residents of states with privacy laws, including California and Washington, have these rights and may appeal a decision by replying to our response.

Children's privacy

TinyTable is made for parents and caregivers, who must be at least 18. A parent or guardian enters all information about their child and agrees to this policy first. We collect only what the app needs, keep it only as long as you keep it in the app, and never share a child's information with third parties for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your information until you delete it or delete the app. Deleted information is removed right away from the app and, once accounts exist, from our servers within 30 days.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards for information about children and health, including encryption, limited access and a written security program. If a breach ever affects your information, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Not medical advice

TinyTable gives general nutrition information, not medical advice. Talk with your child's pediatrician about their health, growth, allergies and supplements.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will show the new version in the app and ask for your agreement before it applies to you.
