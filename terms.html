Using TinyTable

You must be at least 18 and a parent, guardian or caregiver with permission to track the child's eating. You are responsible for the information you enter.

Not medical advice

TinyTable shows estimates from nutrition labels and public databases. Numbers can be incomplete or wrong. It does not diagnose, treat or replace your pediatrician. Always follow your doctor's advice, especially about allergies, supplements, choking risks and food textures, and never delay care because of something in the app. In an emergency, call 911.

Subscriptions

TinyTable Pro starts with a free month, then renews at the price shown ($9.99 a month or $59.99 a year) until you cancel. Payment is charged to your Apple account. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal in your App Store settings. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Recipes and content

Recipes, tips and scores are for general information. Check ingredients for allergies and prepare food safely for your child's age.

Changes and ending

We may update the app or these terms and will tell you about important changes. You can stop using TinyTable and delete your data at any time.
