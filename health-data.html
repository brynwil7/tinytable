Consumer Health Data Notice

Last updated November 11, 2026

What this covers

Some laws, such as Washington's My Health My Data Act, treat food logs, weight, supplements and nutrition results as consumer health data. This notice explains how TinyTable handles that information.

The health data we collect

Your child's weight, birthday, foods eaten, milk, vitamins and supplements, and the nutrient totals, scores and reports TinyTable works out from them.

Why we collect it

To show your child's nutrition, build meal plans and reports, and send the reminders you turn on. Nothing else.

Sharing

We do not sell consumer health data or share it for advertising. It is shared only with service providers that run the app for us, as described in the Privacy Policy, and only with your consent.

Your rights

You can view, download and delete this data in Account → Privacy & data, and withdraw consent at any time. Questions or requests: tinytable.help@gmail.com.
