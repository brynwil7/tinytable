Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice

Effective Date: November 15, 2026
Last Updated: November 17, 2026

This Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice supplements the TinyTable Privacy Policy.

It explains how Tiny Table LLC ("TinyTable," "we," "our," or "us") collects, processes, stores, and shares consumer health data through the TinyTable mobile application.

This Notice is intended to provide additional transparency regarding consumer health data, including information concerning children that parents and authorized adult caregivers enter into the App.

Certain state privacy laws, including Washington's My Health My Data Act, may provide additional rights and protections for consumer health data.

1. What Is Consumer Health Data?

Consumer health data generally refers to personal information that identifies or can reasonably be linked to a consumer and that relates to the consumer's past, present, or future physical or mental health status, subject to the definitions and exclusions in applicable law.

Depending on the information and applicable law, data processed through TinyTable may qualify as consumer health data.

Examples include:

  • Child weight.
  • Feeding and dietary information.
  • Food and milk intake.
  • Vitamin and supplement information.
  • Nutritional calculations.
  • Estimated nutrient consumption.
  • Nutrition reports and summaries.
  • Information that may reveal or be used to infer nutritional or health-related conditions.

We treat this information with care regardless of whether a particular item falls within a specific statutory definition.

2. Categories of Consumer Health Data We Collect

Depending on the features used, TinyTable may collect or process:

Child profile information

  • First name or nickname.
  • Date of birth.
  • Age.
  • Weight.

Feeding and nutrition information

  • Foods consumed.
  • Meal and snack records.
  • Breast milk, formula, and other milk intake.
  • Dietary preferences.
  • Food restrictions or allergies, if entered.
  • Supplement and vitamin information.

Calculated information

  • Estimated nutrient intake.
  • Protein, carbohydrates, fat, and fiber.
  • Vitamins and minerals.
  • Nutrition summaries.
  • Nutritional recommendations generated through App features.

Related information

  • Saved meal plans.
  • Food preferences.
  • Nutrition goals or settings.
  • Information inferred from the information voluntarily entered into the App.

3. How We Collect Consumer Health Data

We collect consumer health data primarily from the adult user who creates and manages a child profile.

Today, this information is stored only on the adult user's device. It is not sent to TinyTable's servers until cloud storage is turned on, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Sources may include:

  • Information entered manually by a parent or authorized caregiver.
  • Food and product information submitted through searches or barcode scans.
  • Nutritional values retrieved from external food databases.
  • Calculations generated from recorded food intake.
  • Preferences and information provided when using meal-planning features.

We may also receive information from authorized adults who contribute to a shared child profile.

4. Purposes for Collecting and Processing Consumer Health Data

TinyTable processes consumer health data to provide the features requested by adult users.

These purposes include:

  • Creating and maintaining child profiles.
  • Tracking food and milk intake.
  • Calculating estimated nutrient consumption.
  • Displaying nutrition summaries and reports.
  • Supporting age-based meal planning.
  • Generating recipe and food suggestions.
  • Supporting dietary preferences and restrictions.
  • Saving meal history and favorites.
  • Synchronizing information across authorized devices.
  • Providing family-sharing functionality.
  • Maintaining account security.
  • Responding to support requests.
  • Complying with legal obligations.

We do not use consumer health data to provide targeted advertising.

5. How Consumer Health Data Is Shared

TinyTable may share consumer health data with service providers when necessary to provide requested App functionality.

Depending on the features used, recipients may include:

Cloud hosting and database providers

These providers may store and process account information, child profiles, food records, and nutritional information to support cloud storage and synchronization.

Provider: Not yet in use. Today all App data is stored only on your device. We will name our cloud database and hosting provider here, and obtain any consent required by law, before cloud storage is turned on.

Artificial intelligence providers

If an adult user requests AI-generated meal suggestions or recommendations, relevant information may be sent to an AI provider to generate the requested result.

Provider: None. TinyTable does not currently use any artificial intelligence provider, and no information is sent to one. If we add AI features, we will name each provider here and obtain any consent required by law before information is sent.

We aim to limit the information shared to what is reasonably necessary for the requested functionality.

Subscription providers

Apple and RevenueCat, if used, may receive subscription-related information. We do not intend to send child nutrition records to these providers for subscription management.

Food and nutrition databases

When an adult searches for food or scans a barcode, the App may transmit the relevant search term or barcode to an external food database.

These requests are intended to retrieve food or product information, not to share a child's complete nutrition profile.

Authorized family members

When an adult account holder enables family sharing, invited adults may access information made available through the shared child profile according to the App's permissions. This includes the child's weight, food and milk logs, supplements and nutrition reports. Section 6 of the Privacy Policy lists what invited adults can and cannot see.

Legal disclosures

We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of TinyTable, our users, or others.

We do not sell consumer health data.

We do not share consumer health data for targeted advertising.

Where applicable law requires consent before sharing consumer health data, we will obtain the required consent before the sharing occurs.

6. Consent

Where required by applicable law, TinyTable obtains consent before collecting or processing consumer health data.

Where required by applicable law, TinyTable obtains separate consent before sharing consumer health data with third parties.

Consent must be informed, voluntary, and sufficiently specific to the processing activity.

A general acceptance of the Privacy Policy or Terms of Use does not automatically replace a separate consent required by law.

Adults may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, subject to applicable legal requirements and the consequences of disabling certain App features. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your data in Account → Privacy & data or by contacting us.

7. Your Consumer Health Data Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Confirm whether TinyTable collects or processes consumer health data.
  • Access consumer health data.
  • Obtain a copy of consumer health data.
  • Request deletion of consumer health data.
  • Withdraw consent to collection or sharing.
  • Request information about categories of consumer health data collected.
  • Request information about purposes for collection and processing.
  • Request information about categories of third parties with whom data is shared.
  • Appeal a denial of a privacy-rights request where applicable.

These rights may be exercised by the adult account holder on their own behalf and, where legally authorized, on behalf of a child whose information they manage.

We will review the appeal and respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

10. Retention and Deletion

We retain consumer health data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Notice, subject to legal requirements.

When a valid deletion request is received, we will delete the relevant consumer health data from our active systems within 30 days and instruct our service providers that hold it on our behalf to delete it too, subject to applicable legal obligations. The full process is described in Section 11 of the Privacy Policy.

Some information may remain temporarily in backups, security logs, or other systems until normal deletion cycles are completed.

11. Children's Information

TinyTable is intended for adults aged 18 and older.

Parents and authorized adult caregivers enter information about children for personal family-management purposes.

Children are not permitted to independently access or operate the App.

The adult account holder is responsible for ensuring they have appropriate authority to submit and manage a child's information.

We may update this Notice as our features, data practices, or legal obligations change.

Material changes may require additional notice or consent.

We will provide notice through the App, email, or another reasonable method where appropriate.
